# Fracta Coral

Fracta Coral is a lamp concept inspired by reef branching, fan coral, and porous marine growth patterns.

## Concept
A pendant shade with upward and outward coral-like branching members that build a porous luminous volume around a central bulb.

## Design goals
- Build in OpenSCAD
- Use BOSL2 where useful
- Design around IKEA-standard bulb, socket, and cable hardware
- Create a stronger volumetric presence than Fern or Canopy
- Preserve airflow and bulb clearance with open branching
