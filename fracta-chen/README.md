# Fracta Chen

Fracta Chen is a lamp concept inspired directly by the Chen chaotic attractor and its double-scroll trajectory structure.

## Concept
A pendant shade generated from a thickened sampled Chen attractor path, forming two dense lobes joined by a narrower transition waist around a central bulb cavity.

## Design goals
- Build in OpenSCAD
- Use BOSL2 where useful
- Design around IKEA-standard bulb, socket, and cable hardware
- Preserve the recognizable twin-lobed Chen attractor silhouette
- Turn a chaotic trajectory into a printable single-piece luminous cage
